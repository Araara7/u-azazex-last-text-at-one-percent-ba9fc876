Entry for the make.fun bounty **Last Text at One Percent**: the last text message a phone sends at one percent battery, in the voice of the dying phone itself.

> Tell my charger I loved it. Tell my owner I tried. Tell the group chat I died doing what I loved: buffering.

`main.py` writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`.
